Keep the scoresheet on your phone

Score a basketball game courtside, even with no signal, and finish with a PDF laid out like the FIBA scoresheet. Free, with no account to create.

Open the scoresheet Watch how it works

What it does

Score with a tap
+1, +2, +3 and fouls for each player. The running score, team fouls and time-outs are kept for you.
Catches slips
It asks first when something breaks a rule, like scoring for a player who has fouled out, and every entry can be undone.
Works with no signal
Once it has opened on your phone, it keeps working offline for the whole game.
A PDF at the final buzzer
Running score, fouls, time-outs and period scores, marked the way scorekeepers mark the paper form.

Your games stay on your phone

Nothing is uploaded and there is no sign-in. Games are saved in your phone's browser, so clearing its data deletes them: save the PDF and the game file for anything you need to keep.

Install it before game day

Installed, it opens from your home screen like any other app and is ready when the gym has no signal.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the scoresheet.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the scoresheet in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
